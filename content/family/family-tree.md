Our research has been ongoing for several years and we are continually adding to Richard Howell's family tree data. We are using WikiTree so it can be available to any and all interested parties free of charge. This allows us to focus this site specifically on Richard Howell and his service to the United States of America. We know WikiTree is the best place to present the family tree data. By clicking on any of these items you will be taken off this site and to the appropriate WikiTree page.
