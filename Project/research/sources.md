## Open Research Questions

### 1. Lewis Howell Death Date — PRIORITY
**The problem:** Three contradictory accounts exist on the site:
- Site profile page: "June 5, 1778"
- Site note: "7 days after the Battle of Monmouth" → July 5, 1778
- Leach (1898): "on the day of the battle of Monmouth" → June 28, 1778

**Analysis:** June 28 (day of Monmouth) and June 5 (three weeks before Monmouth) cannot both be correct. The "7 days after Monmouth" reference would give July 5 — also inconsistent with June 5. The most contemporarily credible source would be regimental records or an obituary notice in a period newspaper.

**To resolve:** Check regimental muster returns for June–July 1778; check period NJ/PA newspapers for obituary notices; check Elmer's diary (a Cumberland County contemporary who knew the Howells).

**Current status:** June 5, 1778 used on site with caveat note on timeline page. DO NOT present any date as definitive until verified.

### 2. Clarence Parvin — Greenwich Tea Burning
**The problem:** The name "Clarence Parvin" appears in the list of Greenwich Tea Burning participants on the site, but this name does not appear prominently in other consulted sources. The other names on the list are well-documented in period records.

**Analysis:** "Parvin" is a known Cumberland County family name. However "Clarence" as a first name is unusual for the 1770s colonial period — it became more common in the 19th century. The original name may have been garbled in transcription. Possible alternatives: "Caleb Parvin" or another Parvin family member.

**To resolve:** Cross-reference against *Tea Burners of Cumberland County* document; check Cumberland County records for Parvin family members active in 1774.

**Current status:** Name used on site as-is with internal flag. Requires verification before content freeze.

### 3. The Patriotic Song for Washington — OPEN
**The problem:** The site states that Richard Howell wrote a patriotic song sung for General Washington at a Trenton reception during Washington's inaugural journey (April 1789). The song has not been identified.

**To resolve:** Check period newspapers (NJ Gazette, Pennsylvania Packet) for coverage of Washington's Trenton reception in April 1789; check Howell family papers if any survive; check period anthologies of patriotic verse.

**Current status:** Unresolved. Not a launch blocker.
