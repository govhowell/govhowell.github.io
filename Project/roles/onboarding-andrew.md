## The Project

We are migrating the govhowell.org website — currently a messy static WordPress export — into a clean, well-structured Hugo-based static site deployable on GitHub Pages. The site is a historical reference dedicated to **Richard Howell (1754–1802)**, Governor of New Jersey and officer in the American Revolutionary War.

Your role is the most critical for long-term quality: you are the historical authority on this project. Nothing goes live that hasn't passed your review.
